Get started

This takes about five minutes. There's nothing to install. Everything runs in your web browser.

  1. Sign in. Open the 📊 Dashboard and sign in with the email and password IT gave you.
  2. Link your Network ID. The first time you sign in, a box asks for your Aqua Network User ID, for example jdoe. It fills in a guess from your email. This ID becomes the address of your Briefing and Status Report.
  3. Change your password. Open the Settings tab, go to Change Password, and replace the password from IT.
  4. Add your name and department. On the same tab, fill in Display Name and Department, then click Save Settings. Your name then shows on the Status Report in place of your ID.
  5. Add your projects. On the Active Projects tab, click + Add New Project. See Add or edit an item.

If the setup box warns that your ID already has a briefing, stop. That briefing may belong to someone else, and linking to it would let you edit their report. Only continue if the ID really is yours. If you're not sure, email ITHelpdesk@aqua-aerobic.com.

The minimum: keep your Active Projects up to date. They feed the Weekly Status Report and the charts. Quick Tasks and Daily Tasks are optional.

The 📊 Dashboard has four tabs, in the same order your Briefing shows them.

Add or edit an item

  1. Click + Add New Project or + Add New Quick Task. To change an item you already have, click Edit on its row.
  2. Fill in at least the Name, Status, and Priority. Everything else is optional.
  3. Click Save. Open or refresh your Briefing to see the change.

Put items in order

  • Click Up or Down on a row to move it. The Status Report can use this order (pick My dashboard order there).
  • Click ↕ Sort by Status to sort the whole tab by status in one step. Saving an item never re-sorts your list.
  • Click Delete to remove an item. You'll be asked to confirm.

Search your items

  • Type in the search box under the tabs. It searches all three lists at once: names, status, priority, people, goals, notes, milestones, attachment names, comments, and dates.
  • Every word you type has to match. chuck publish finds items that mention both words.
  • Each tab shows how many items match. Up and Down are turned off while you search. Click Clear or press Esc to see everything again.

🎯 Steering Focus: rank your top projects

Steering Focus is your ranked list of the projects the steering committee should look at first. You can rank up to 20 projects. #1 is the most important.

Your focus projects show up in two places:

  • At the top of your Weekly Status Report, in the 🎯 Steering Focus block.
  • In Team Hub, which shows each person's top 3.

Set, move, or remove a number

Use the 🎯 dropdown on the project's row in the Dashboard. You don't need to open Edit. The edit window has the same dropdown.

To do thisPickWhat happens to the others
Add a project➕ Add as Focus #NProjects at that number and below move down one.
Move a project🎯 Focus #NThe projects in between shift by one to make room.
Remove a project🎯 Not a focus projectThe projects below it move up one.

Example. Your list is #1 Website, #2 CRM, #3 Shipping Report. On Shipping Report, pick 🎯 Focus #1. Your list is now #1 Shipping Report, #2 Website, #3 CRM.

Rules

  • Numbers never skip. Remove #2 and the old #3 becomes #2.
  • Marking a project Completed, or deleting it, takes it off the list.
  • Once 20 projects are ranked, you can still move them, but you can't add another until one comes off.
  • To clear the whole list at once, use Reset Focus Rankings on the Settings tab.
  • Team Hub admins can also set your numbers. Their changes show on your Dashboard right away.

🏁 Milestones and roadblocks

Split a project into milestones so people can see how far along it is. Reports show progress as, for example, "2/5 complete".

  1. Open the project and find 🏁 Milestones. Click + Add Milestone.
  2. Enter a Base Complete date (the original plan) and an Est/Act Complete date (your current estimate, or the date it actually finished).
  3. Tick the box at the left of the milestone when it's done.
  • In Progress and On-Hold flags show what you're working on now and what's paused. Give a reason when you put one on hold.
  • 🚧 Roadblock: tick it, and give a reason, when something blocks the milestone. It shows red on the reports and moves the project to the top of Key Projects.
  • Use ▲ and ▼ to put milestones in order.
  • When every milestone is done but the project isn't marked Completed, the Status Report lists it under 🏁 Ready to close. Click its ✔ button to mark it Completed.

📋 Daily operational checks

Use these for systems or routines you check every day. They work on any project or quick task.

  1. Open the item and find 📋 Daily Operational Check.
  2. Pick a result: ✅ Verified (all good), ⚠️ Issues Found (add a note saying what's wrong; the note is required), or ⚪ Not Checked.
  3. Click Log Check. Each check is saved under Check History, so you can see how the system has held up over time.

🏠 Your Briefing page

Your Briefing is the read-only view of your work. Anyone with the link can open it without signing in.

Share it

  • Your link is https://dailyaquabriefing.github.io/?report=YOUR_ID. Replace YOUR_ID with your Network ID, for example ?report=jdoe.
  • While you're signed in, 🏠 My Briefing in the navigation bar opens your own.

Read it

  • Active Projects opens expanded. Quick Tasks and Daily Tasks start closed. Click a section heading to open or close it, or use ➕ Expand All and ➖ Collapse All.
  • Active Projects and Quick Tasks have a row of status buttons, such as In Progress (4). Click one to show only items with that status. Click ALL to see everything again.
  • Badges point out recent work: ✨ Updated Today means the item was changed or commented on today. 🔄 Updated Recently means within the last 7 days.

Comments

  • Anyone can click 💬 Add Question/Comment on an item. Once comments exist, the button reads 💬 View/Add Comments with a count.
  • You read and delete comments in the Dashboard, in the item's edit window.

Charts

Click 📊 Analytics in the bar at the top of the Briefing. You get four charts: Project & Task Status, Priority Breakdown, Workload Distribution, and Project Milestones. A table under them shows each project's milestone progress and its next milestone, and marks overdue, roadblocked, and on-hold milestones in red.

Export to Excel

  • Click 📥 Export to Excel. The first sheet is an Analytics Overview, followed by one sheet each for Projects, Quick Tasks, and Daily Tasks.
  • The file includes Assigned To, every milestone, and the full daily check history. Columns are sized to fit and formatting is removed so the cells read cleanly.
  • The file also makes a good backup of your work.

📋 Weekly Status Report

The Status Report builds a steering-committee report from your briefing. There's nothing extra to type. Just keep your Active Projects current.

Make your report

  1. Click 📋 Team Status in the navigation bar. If you're signed in, it opens your own report. You can also open weekly-report.html?report=YOUR_ID.
  2. Optional: click ⚙ Options to choose sections, filters, and the project order.
  3. Share it: click Save PDF, Download web report, or Copy as email text.

What's in it

  • 🎯 Steering Focus: your ranked focus projects. It shows when the Key Projects section is on.
  • Completion %: how far along your projects are overall.
  • Status of Projects: how many projects are in each status. Click a status to show only those projects.
  • Key Projects: a table with each project's status, milestones, owner, and dates.
  • On Hold, Quick Tasks, Daily Workload, Maintenance, and Recently Completed (including 🏁 Ready to close).

Status codes in the tables: C = complete, G = in progress and on track, T = in testing, Y = on hold, delayed, or blocked, N/S = not started.

A red "Last updated N days ago" warning means that briefing hasn't changed in more than 7 days. Update any item in the Dashboard to clear it.

More options

Choose which sections appear

Click ⚙ Options. The Sections row has a checkbox for each section: Completion %, Status of Projects, Key Projects, On Hold, Quick Tasks, Daily Workload, Maintenance, and Recently Completed. Untick what you don't need.

The PDF, web download, and email text use the same choices, and your choices are remembered for next time.

Filter projects and set their order
  • Filters (under ⚙ Options): status, priority (including High + Medium together), what needs attention (needs attention, due within 30 days, overdue, no est. date), and how far back to show completed work (last 7 days up to all time).
  • Order:
    • Needs attention first: delayed and roadblocked projects first, then by priority, then by date.
    • My dashboard order: the order you set with Up and Down on your Dashboard.
    • Custom (Projects panel): the order you set in the Projects panel.
  • 📂 Projects… opens a list of every project in the report. Untick a project to hide it, and use ▲ and ▼ to set a custom order. The report notes how many projects are hidden.
Put several people in one report
  • Type several Network IDs separated by commas, for example jdoe, msmith. Leads and admins can also pick names from the Add user… dropdown.
  • Each person gets their own section, and the PDF starts each person on a new page. Use ▲ and ▼ on the name chips to change the order, and × to remove someone.
  • Tick Merge into one report to combine everyone into one completion %, one status summary, and one Key Projects table. A small tag on each row shows whose project it is.
  • A link like weekly-report.html?report=jdoe,msmith opens the same group directly.
Saved teams
  • Open a team: anyone can pick one from the 👥 Teams… dropdown, or open a link like weekly-report.html?team=Engineering.
  • Create a team (department leads and admins, signed in): load the people you want, click 💾 Create Team, and give it a name. With a team open, the button reads 💾 Save team and updates that team.
  • A team remembers its members, the Merge setting, the sections, the order, and your Projects panel choices, so it opens exactly the same way every time.
  • Remove a team on the 🔐 Admin Panel page.
Save your own report layout

Click 💾 Save Personal Report to keep your sections, order, and Projects panel choices with your report. It then opens the same way on any computer.

The button only works when you're signed in and looking at your own report. It never changes anyone else's report, and it doesn't apply to team reports.

Viewing and sharing tools
  • ⛶ Full width stretches the report across the screen. The setting is remembered.
  • ⤢ Full screen is for presenting. Press Esc to leave it.
  • Save PDF, Download web report, and Copy as email text share the report.
  • Can't connect? Drop a Briefing_….xlsx file (from 📥 Export to Excel) onto the report's start screen to build the report from that file.

👥 Team Hub (for managers)

Team Hub puts everyone's projects on one board for management review. It runs on its own site, managerhub.aquatools.app.

  • Sign in with your Windows username (for example jsmith) and your normal computer password. You stay signed in for a year on that browser. On the office network, http://aqua94:5096/ also works with the one-click Sign in with Windows button.
  • Access: your first sign-in gives you read-only access and emails ckonkol@aqua-aerobic.com. The manager role adds comments, P1/P2/P3 priorities, project ideas, and chat. The admin role adds the Admin panel.
  • What you see: every project with its owner, status, and milestones; search and filters; a Risk & Focus board of delayed, roadblocked, and overdue projects; and each person's top 3 Steering Focus projects.
  • Your briefing stays yours. Comments and priorities in Team Hub never change anyone's briefing. The one exception: admins can set a person's Steering Focus numbers.
  • For full instructions, see the Team Hub help page.

Questions and fixes

I forgot my password.

Email ITHelpdesk@aqua-aerobic.com or ask your department lead. They can reset it from the Admin Panel.

The Up and Down buttons are grayed out.

The search box has text in it. Click Clear or press Esc, and the buttons come back.

I can't add another focus project.

The focus list holds 20 projects. Remove one (🎯 Not a focus project) or mark one Completed, then add the new one.

A project dropped off my Steering Focus list.

Projects leave the list when they're marked Completed or deleted, or when someone clicks Reset Focus Rankings. A Team Hub admin may also have changed it. The other projects move up to fill the gap.

My change doesn't show on my Briefing.

Make sure you clicked Save in the edit window, then refresh the Briefing page.

The 💾 Save Personal Report button is grayed out.

It only works when you're signed in and looking at your own report. Sign in on the Dashboard, then click 📋 Team Status in the navigation bar.

I don't see the 💾 Create Team button.

Only department leads and admins can create teams, and they have to be signed in. Anyone can open a saved team from the 👥 Teams… dropdown.

The report shows a red "Last updated N days ago" warning.

That briefing hasn't changed in more than 7 days. Update any item in the Dashboard and the warning goes away.

The setup box says my ID already has a briefing.

Don't link to it unless it really is your Network ID. Linking would let you edit that person's report. If you're not sure, email ITHelpdesk@aqua-aerobic.com.
