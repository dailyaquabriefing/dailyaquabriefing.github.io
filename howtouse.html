How to Use Aqua Daily Briefing

👋 Say goodbye to juggling multiple apps! The Aqua Daily Briefing is your personalized daily co-pilot. It combines your Daily Tasks, Projects, and Active Tasks into one secure, shareable dashboard — plus a one-click Weekly Status Report for the steering committee.

Finding Your Way Around NEW

Every page shares a navigation bar at the top: 🏠 Briefing, Dashboard, Status Report, Help, and Users (IT). Once you open your briefing, the bar remembers your User ID and keeps the Briefing and Status Report links pointed at your data.

Step 1: Admin Dashboard & First-Time Login

Use the Admin Dashboard to manage what appears on your report. There is no software to install — everything runs in the browser.

  1. Go to the Admin Dashboard (or click Dashboard in the navigation bar).
  2. Logging In: Sign in with the email and default password provided by IT.
  3. First Time? After your first sign-in, you'll be prompted to link your account to your Aqua Network User ID (e.g., jdoe). That ID becomes your report address, and your (empty) briefing and status report pages start working immediately.
  4. Recommended: change the default password right away on the Settings tab (see "Change Password" below).

Step 2: Managing Your Content

The Admin Dashboard is organized into four tabs. Content supports Rich Text (Bold, Bullets, Headers) via the new editor.

1. Daily Tasks Tab

  • Add your top 3-5 focus items for the day.
  • Use the Up/Down buttons to prioritize them.

2. Projects & Active Tasks Tabs

Minimal usage suggestion: at a minimum, keep your Active Projects up to date — they are what feeds the Weekly Status Report (Key Projects, On Hold, completion %). Daily Tasks and Active Tasks are optional.

These sections support rich data. When adding or editing an item:

  • Rich Text Notes NEW: Use the editor to format your notes with bullet points, bold text, and headers.
  • Multiple Attachments NEW: You can now upload multiple files or add manual links (e.g., SharePoint URLs) to a single task.
  • Assigned To NEW: Pick who owns the item from the dropdown, or choose "Other" to type a name. Unassigned items show as N/A on the Weekly Report.
  • Neutral statuses NEW: Alongside the original stages, items can be Planning, In Progress, or Review — friendlier wording for non-IT departments.
  • Collaborators: Tag teams (e.g., "Design Team") to display a yellow Team badge.
  • Milestones IMPROVED: Add multiple milestones (🏁) per item, each with a Base Complete date, an Est/Act Complete date, and In Progress / On-Hold flags (with an on-hold reason). Check 🚧 Roadblock on a milestone (with a reason) to flag it as blocked — it shows red on the reports and pushes the project to the top of Key Projects. Reorder milestones with the ▲/▼ buttons on each row. Progress like "2/5 complete" shows on the report.
  • Dates: Set Start and End dates. The Last Updated field updates automatically.
  • Private Comments NEW: Private notes for internal use.

3. Settings Tab (Security)

  • Profile NEW: Set your Display Name and Department — they replace your user ID on the Weekly Status Report header and group you correctly in team reports.
  • Passcode Protection: Set a passcode here to lock your Daily Briefing view.
  • Important: You must set a passcode to enable the "Private Comments" field.
  • Change Password NEW: Update your dashboard sign-in password yourself — enter your current password, the new one twice, and click Change Password. Do this after your first login to replace the default password from IT. (Forgot your password? IT can reset it from the Users page.)

Step 3: Daily Operational Checks NEW

Track the health of your systems or routine tasks directly within any Project or Active Task.

  • How to Log: Open any item in the Admin Dashboard. Look for the "📋 Daily Operational Check" box.
  • Status Options:
    • ✅ Verified: System is healthy.
    • ⚠️ Issues Found: Requires attention (Note required).
    • ⚪ Not Checked: Placeholder status.
  • History: A log of previous checks is saved automatically, so you can track stability over time.

Step 4: Smart Visual Indicators NEW

The dashboard now visually highlights fresh content so viewers know what's new.

  • ✨ Updated Today: Appears on items edited or commented on today.
  • 🔄 Updated Recently: Appears on items active within the last 7 days.
  • No Badge: Items older than 7 days appear standard.

Step 5: Public & Private Comments

Stakeholders can ask questions, and you can keep private notes secure.

  • Public Comments: Viewers can click "💬 View/Add Comments" to chat. The new editor supports Rich Text formatting.
  • Private Comments: Available in the Admin Edit modal. These notes are hidden from the public and only visible when the Report Passcode is entered.

Step 6: Export to Excel IMPROVED

Need a hard copy? The export feature has been upgraded.

  • Where: Click 📥 Export to Excel in the top nav.
  • What's New:
    • Includes the Assigned To column and the full Milestones list.
    • Includes the full Daily Check History log.
    • Includes Analytics Overview as the first sheet.
    • Automatically strips HTML for clean, readable text cells.
    • Adjusts column widths automatically for readability.
  • Smart Security:
    • If exported from the Public View, it excludes Private Comments.
    • If exported from the Private (Unlocked) View, it includes Private Comments.

Step 7: Visual Analytics

Switch instantly between list view and visual charts.

  • How to Access: Click "📊 Analytics" in the top nav.
  • Charts Included:
    1. Project Status: Doughnut chart of On Track vs Delayed.
    2. Priority Breakdown: Bar chart of High/Med/Low tasks.
    3. Workload Distribution: Pie chart of how work is split across Daily/Projects/Active Tasks.

Step 8: Viewing & Sharing Reports

You have three views available based on your needs:

🔒 Daily Briefing (Private)

URL: /?daily=YOUR_ID

  • Includes: Tasks, Projects, Active Tasks, and Private Comments.
  • Security: Protected by your Passcode.

📊 Weekly Report (Public)

URL: /?report=YOUR_ID

  • Includes: Tasks, Projects, Resources, and Public Comments.
  • No Sensitive Data: Hides Private Comments.

📋 Weekly Status Report Builder NEW

URL: weekly-report.html?report=YOUR_ID (or click Status Report in the navigation bar)

  • Minimum to get value: the report is built mainly from your Active Projects, so keep those current. Daily Tasks (shown as Recurring Daily Workload) and Active Tasks are optional — hide their sections with the toolbar toggles if you don't use them.
  • Builds a steering-committee style status report straight from your live briefing data: overall completion %, status chips, Key Projects with milestones and Assigned To, On Hold, Active Tasks, Recurring Daily Workload, and Recently Completed.
  • Multiple users NEW: Enter several User IDs separated by commas (e.g. jdoe, msmith), or pick a name from the "Add user…" dropdown in the toolbar — it lists everyone with a briefing and adds them the moment you select. Each user's report appears as its own section; use the ▲/▼ buttons on the user chips to set the order (× removes one). The PDF starts each user on a new page. weekly-report.html?report=jdoe,msmith auto-connects the whole team.
  • Saved teams NEW: After loading a group of users, click 💾 Save team in the toolbar and give it a name (e.g. "Engineering"). A team saves its members, the "Merge into one report" setting, and the section checkboxes — opening the team restores all three. With a team open, Save team prefills its name so you update it in place. Saved teams appear as one-click chips on the connect screen and home page, and weekly-report.html?team=Engineering opens one directly. The "Add user…" dropdown is grouped by department and shows display names.
  • Freshness warnings NEW: If a briefing hasn't been updated in over 7 days, its report section (and the PDF) shows a red "Last updated N days ago" warning so stale numbers are obvious.
  • Merge into one report NEW: With two or more users loaded, check "Merge into one report" in the toolbar to combine everyone into a single report — one completion %, one status band, and one Key Projects table sorted across the whole team. Each row gets a small tag showing whose briefing it came from. Uncheck to go back to one section per user.
  • Filters NEW: Tap a status chip, or filter by priority and focus (needs attention, due within 30 days, overdue, no est. date).
  • Toolbar NEW: Filters, user chips, and section toggles live under the ⚙ Options button (collapsed by default). ⛶ Full width stretches the report across the screen (remembered), and ⤢ Full screen presents it (Esc to exit).
  • Show/hide sections NEW: The Sections row in the toolbar (under ⚙ Options) has a checkbox per section (Completion %, Status of Projects, Key Projects, On Hold, Active Tasks, Daily Workload, Maintenance, Recently Completed). Uncheck what you don't want — the PDF, web download, and copied email text follow the same choices, and they're remembered for next time.
  • Outputs: Download as a PDF, download a standalone web page, or copy as email text. An .xlsx export can also be dropped on the page as an offline fallback.
